## Platform

web

## Users

Congregation members looking for worship schedules and church announcements, and first-time visitors looking for the church location and general information.

## Product Purpose

Provide a web presence for Jemaat Bukit Pengharapan where congregation members and prospective visitors can find worship schedules, announcements, and general church information. Success means these audiences can find the information they need easily.

## Positioning

No distinct positioning or mechanism has been confirmed.

## Operating Context

The site serves both returning congregation members checking current information and first-time visitors seeking practical church details. Exact workflows and content-maintenance responsibilities have not been established.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed priorities: worship schedules, church announcements, general church information, and a public contact channel.
- The homepage provides portal entries for Profil, Jadwal, Renungan, Galeri, and Kontak. Public routes are implemented for Profil, Jadwal Ibadah, Warta & Berita, and Pelayanan; verified content for those pages is still pending.
- The homepage should highlight several recent Warta & Berita posts in an ordered visual layout and provide a gallery area for majelis photos when official entries are available.
- The dedicated Kontak page provides a validated message form and a Google Maps location embed.
- The admin dashboard supports editing future worship schedules, storing and tracking contact messages, and managing separate admin accounts without public self-registration.
- One admin utama manages pengelola accounts; all active admins share the inbox and can assign message follow-up.
- The application uses CodeIgniter 4 with Bootstrap 5.3.3 served from local assets.
- The interface must be accessible and responsive for both congregation members and first-time visitors. The required accessibility standard is undecided.
- Content language and deployment constraints are undecided.
